## Purpose

This project is a small documentation study for power distribution basics: load schedules, one-line diagrams, voltage drop, panel schedules, and maintenance notes.

## Planned Scope

- Define a small commercial load scenario.
- Build a load schedule.
- Draw a one-line diagram.
- Estimate voltage drop.
- Select protective devices at a learning level.
- Write a maintenance checklist for technician inspection.

## Note

Any real installation must follow applicable codes and be reviewed by qualified professionals.
